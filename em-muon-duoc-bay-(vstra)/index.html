VSTRA: Em Muon Duoc Bay
A high-energy music video blending streetwear aesthetics with surreal 3D visual storytelling.
Index

Official Music Video.


Type
      Music Video, Art Direction, 3D Modeling, Production

Year
      2026

Role
      3D Concept Modeling Artist

Studios
      The Tripod Guys & Antiantiart (Production)
      Vidua Studio (Creative)     The Context

    Sponsored by streetwear brand Levents, "Em Muon Duoc Bay" is a visionary music video for Vietnamese artist VSTRA. The project demands a futuristic and fashion-forward visual language, merging surreal digital environments with highly stylized physical sets to reflect the artist's bold identity.

The Execution & Role

    Operating in a highly dynamic, cross-disciplinary production environment, my role bridged physical art direction and digital world-building.

    Working closely with the Directors and the Art Department, I assisted in translating complex creative concepts into tangible sets and bespoke props (including custom roller skates and luggage). Simultaneously, I contributed to the 3D modeling process, ensuring a seamless visual flow and strict aesthetic consistency between the physical shoot and the final CGI integrations.
