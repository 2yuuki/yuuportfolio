Vietjet Air: Enjoy Flying
A vibrant, high-energy commercial campaign celebrating the joy of travel and modern aviation.
Index


Official Commercial Video.


Type
      Commercial Video, Post-Production

Year
      2024

Role
      Post Producer

Agencies
      Cheil Vietnam (Agency)
      OneCut (Production House)     The Context

    Vietjet Air's "Enjoy Flying" is a major commercial campaign aimed at revitalizing the joy of travel. The project required a highly dynamic visual narrative, combining live-action choreography, intricate set designs, and extensive post-production to deliver a premium, engaging brand message across multiple digital and broadcast platforms.

The Execution & Role

    Operating as the Post Producer for OneCut, I was responsible for architecting and overseeing the entire post-production pipeline. Collaborating with an international directing team from Parallax Collective and various post-vendors, I managed the offline editing, color grading, CGI/VFX integration, and sound design.

    The role demanded rigorous quality control, cross-team communication, and agile timeline management to seamlessly deliver multiple high-fidelity asset variations for a major airline brand.
