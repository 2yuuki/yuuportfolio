TikTok Shop 3.3 Mega Sale
A high-volume, fast-paced commercial campaign optimized for algorithm-driven social platforms.
Index




Type
      Commercial Video, Production, Post-Production

Year
      2025

Role
      Producer & Post-Producer

Agencies
      Climax Creatology & ZEE (Creative)
      OneCut (Production)     The Context

    TikTok Shop 3.3 "San Deal Sieu Vui" is a major e-commerce mega sale campaign requiring a massive output of digital assets. The project demanded extreme agility to capture the vibrant, trend-driven energy of TikTok while maintaining rigorous brand guidelines across both the main commercial film and a wide array of social media deliverables.

The Execution & Role

    Operating as both Producer (for social assets) and Post-Producer (for the main film), I managed a high-velocity production pipeline. I coordinated cross-functional teams to deliver over 20 digital assets within a tight 20-day turnaround.

    My focus was on optimizing the post-production workflow, ensuring strict quality control (QC), and translating raw footage into high-converting, platform-native variations that meet the intense demands of algorithmic social media environments.
