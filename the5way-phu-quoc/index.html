The 5Way Phu Quoc
A high-end commercial campaign introducing a pioneering 5-in-1 coastal lifestyle concept.
Index


Official Commercial Video.


Type
      TVC, Creative, Commercial Production

Year
      2023

Role
      Creative (Pitching & Concept Development)

Agency
      OneCut (Production House)     The Context

    "The 5Way Phu Quoc - Life Concepts" is a massive real estate campaign by VinGroup, introducing a pioneering coastal apartment model within the Grand World complex. The project demanded a luxurious and highly persuasive visual narrative to launch its 5-in-1 core values-Live, Experience, Relax, Invest, and Safe-targeting both premium investors and modern lifestyle seekers.

The Execution & Role

    Operating as a Creative at OneCut Production House, I was instrumental in developing the strategic visual treatment and storytelling approach that successfully secured the multi-billion VND pitch.

    Working closely with the Director and the production team, I helped translate complex real estate selling points into an engaging, high-end commercial film. My focus was on ensuring that Vinhomes' premium brand identity and the "Life Concepts" vision were flawlessly executed from the initial storyboard to the final camera lens.
