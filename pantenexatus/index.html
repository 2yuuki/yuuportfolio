Pantene x Anh Tu Atus
A high-impact social campaign blending celebrity influence with dynamic commercial production.
Index




Type
      Social Campaign, Commercial Video

Year
      2025

Role
      Creative Lead

Agencies
      Ecomobi x OneCut (Creative & Production)     The Context

    Partnering with Vietnamese celebrity Anh Tu Atus, Pantene launched a major social media campaign targeting young digital audiences. The project required a vibrant, high-energy visual narrative that could capture attention in a fast-scrolling digital landscape while strictly upholding Pantene's established global brand standards.

The Execution & Role

    Operating as the Creative Lead at OneCut, my role was to conceptualize and steer the overall visual direction of the campaign. 

    Working in a fast-paced environment, I collaborated closely with the Director and the production crew to ensure the creative vision was flawlessly translated from the physical set to the final social media assets. This required balancing celebrity branding with high-volume digital production demands, ensuring aesthetic consistency across all deliverables.
