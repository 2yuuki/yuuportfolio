Agencies

      Command+Art (Production)     The Context

   For Cocoon's Lotus Micellar Water launch, the campaign paired Vietnamese lotus imagery with a modern visual language and Phuong My Chi.

The Execution & Role

   As Director's Assistant with an Art Direction focus, I coordinated set builds, lotus-inspired motifs, and miniature setups while maintaining visual consistency across a tight shoot.
