Nguyen Thu Trang (yuuwouldnever) is a Vietnamese multidisciplinary visual designer whose practice investigates the beauty of everyday life through functional visual works. She makes work that serves a purpose: to open reflection, communicate social conditions, or give form to inner experience. Her creative process is introspective yet communicative.

VSTRA: Em Muon Duoc Bay
A high-energy music video blending streetwear aesthetics with surreal 3D visual storytelling.
Index

Official Music Video.


Type
      Music Video, Art Direction, 3D Modeling, Production

Year
      2026

Role
      3D Concept Modeling Artist

Studios
      The Tripod Guys & Antiantiart (Production)
      Vidua Studio (Creative)     The Context

    VSTRA's "Em Muon Duoc Bay," sponsored by Levents, combines stylized sets with surreal digital environments and a fashion-led visual direction.

The Execution & Role

    I bridged physical art direction and digital world-building, developing sets and custom props while contributing to the 3D modeling used in the final CGI integrations.
