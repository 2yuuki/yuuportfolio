Sau Con Suy -Rhyder
A culturally rich music video project blending contemporary storytelling with authentic on-location set design.
Index

Official Music Video.


Type
      Music Video, Art Direction, Production

Year
     

Role
      Assistant Art Director

Studios
      The Red Team (Production)     The Context

    Shot in Ban Cang, Phu Tho, "Sau Con Suy" grounds its contemporary narrative in local materials and tactile set design.

The Execution & Role

    As Assistant Art Director, I sourced props, dressed practical locations, and solved on-set spatial challenges while protecting the visual direction during an unpredictable outdoor shoot.
