Nguyen Thu Trang (yuuwouldnever) is a Vietnamese multidisciplinary visual designer whose practice investigates the beauty of everyday life through functional visual works. She makes work that serves a purpose: to open reflection, communicate social conditions, or give form to inner experience. Her creative process is introspective yet communicative.

Role

Production
      Brunchie Asia     The Context

    "Doiii" by Hau Hoang featuring MisThy combines energetic choreography, transforming sets, and a playful graphic language.

The Execution & Role

    As Director's Assistant and Lead Graphic Designer, I coordinated the shoot with Director Kha Bao and developed the core graphic assets that connect the film's sets and digital identity.
