The 5Way Phu Quoc
A high-end commercial campaign introducing a pioneering 5-in-1 coastal lifestyle concept.
Index


Official Commercial Video.


Type
      TVC, Creative, Commercial Production

Year
      2023

Role
      Creative (Pitching & Concept Development)

Agency
      OneCut (Production House)     The Context

    "The 5Way Phu Quoc – Life Concepts" introduces VinGroup's coastal apartment model through five values: Live, Experience, Relax, Invest, and Safe.

The Execution & Role

    As a Creative at OneCut, I developed the visual treatment and storytelling approach that secured the pitch, then helped translate the selling points into a premium commercial aligned with Vinhomes' brand identity.
