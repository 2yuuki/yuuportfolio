APL 2026: Queens Showdown
A cinematic esports trailer introducing the queens of APL 2026 through a high-impact visual showdown.
Index


Official trailer.


Type
      Esports Trailer, Graphic Design, VFX

Year
      2026

Role
      Graphic Designer

Client
      Garena

Production House
      FGS - Production The Context

    APL 2026: Queens Showdown introduces the tournament through a cinematic clash between its queens, combining live action, esports energy, and a dense VFX-driven world.

The Execution & Role

    As Graphic Designer, I created graphic assets for the trailer and worked within the production and post-production pipeline to keep the visual language consistent with the campaign's competitive, high-impact direction.
