Cocoon: Finding The Healing

Role
      Art Director

Agency
      Command+Art (Production)     The Context

    "Finding The Healing" translates Cocoon's focus on self-care and natural restoration into a calm visual world featuring Minh Thuy.

The Execution & Role

    As Art Director, I shaped the set, palette, and natural elements, then worked with the Director and production team to keep the soothing visual language consistent on camera.
