Hear the Wind Sing
A book cover design study exploring two visual directions through illustration, lettering, and typographic pacing.
Index




Type
      Book cover design, typography, lettering, publication design

Year
      2024

Reference
      Hear the Wind Sing by Haruki Murakami
      Vietnamese edition cover

Methods
      Cover analysis, typographic composition, custom illustration, lettering, layout studies, visual pacing

Showcase
      CHOROTYPE exhibition showcase     Overview

    For this project, I wanted to see how the same book could feel completely different through two cover directions: one expressive and image-led, the other quieter and built only with type. I used the Vietnamese edition as my starting point, then reworked its atmosphere through composition, rhythm, and texture.  





Design Question     I kept one question in mind: how much can a reader’s first impression change when the same story is introduced through an image-led cover versus a type-led cover?  

Context     I began by looking closely at the original Vietnamese edition—its color, atmosphere, and pacing. I did not want to reproduce it. I used it as a reference point while testing what illustration could communicate that typography could not, and vice versa.  


Version 1     In Version 1, I combined custom illustration and lettering in a layered composition. I wanted the cover to feel active and slightly unpredictable, as if the atmosphere of the story was moving across the surface.  

Version 2     For Version 2, I removed the illustration and worked with typography alone. Contrast, spacing, pacing, and texture had to carry the whole mood, which made this direction quieter but more controlled.  


Outcome     Putting the two covers side by side made the difference clear: the visual system changes how the same book is approached before a page is even opened. The work was later presented at the CHOROTYPE showcase.  


Reflection     This project helped me understand cover design as a process of translation. I do not need to summarize the story literally; I need to create the right atmosphere for someone to want to enter it.
