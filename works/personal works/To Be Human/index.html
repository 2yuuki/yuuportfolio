To be human
A 2D-3D visual jockey piece exploring the paradox of emotion and existence through a cyborg's pursuit of humanity.
Index





Type
      Motion Graphics, 2D-3D Visual Jockey

Year
      2023

Role
      Director, Look Developer, 3D Modeler & Sound Designer

View Research Document     The Insight & Narrative

    "To be human" is a philosophical inquiry into emotion, memory, and artificial existence. The narrative follows a cyborg relentlessly chasing the concept of "humanity" to understand the meaning of its own creation. Moving through various emotional stages, the cyborg ultimately discovers that its target is just another machine caught in the exact same pursuit.

    Rather than providing a direct resolution, the project presents a paradox: the endless loop of longing, doubting, and searching for meaning is, in fact, the very definition of being human.

The Execution & Role

    Operating as the Director and Lead Multidisciplinary Designer, I constructed this visual jockey piece by seamlessly integrating 3D character modeling with 2D graphic motion.

    I developed the entire visual pipeline—from storyboard and look development to sound design and final editing. The project demonstrates a rigorous technical workflow, utilizing motion, sound, and visual pacing to translate a complex existential concept into a high-fidelity, looping sensory experience.
