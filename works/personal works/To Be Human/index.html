To be human
A 2D-3D visual jockey piece exploring the paradox of emotion and existence through a cyborg's pursuit of humanity.
Index





Type
      Motion Graphics, 2D-3D Visual Jockey

Year
      2023

Role
      Director, Look Developer, 3D Modeler & Sound Designer

Research Document below     The Insight & Narrative

    The project began while we were listening to two cyberpunk tracks. Their strong rhythm immediately made us imagine an arcade-like chase through a chaotic futuristic city. From there, we developed a cyborg who is chasing his own “humanity,” believing that catching it will give him real emotions and explain why he was created.

    He passes through fear, sadness, anger, and happiness, only to discover that his target is another cyborg chasing someone else. The search begins again, turning the desire to feel human into an endless loop.

The Execution & Role

    As Director and Lead Multidisciplinary Designer, I worked across 3D design and animation, sound, effects, and live VJ mapping. We built a different visual world for each emotion: eyes and snakes for fear, broken droids and memory-card graves for sadness, burning televisions for anger, and a colourful collage tunnel for happiness.

    VJ was new to me at the beginning, but it quickly became the part that excited me most. I learned that the work was not only about making strong visuals; it also had to respond to the music, the venue, the atmosphere, and the audience in real time.
