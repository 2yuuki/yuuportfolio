Kieu
A speculative 3D character design exploring Vietnamese diaspora identity, historical dress, and contemporary fashion remix.
Index


Final render of the character.


Type
      3D character design, speculative fashion, cultural remix

Year
      2023

Role
      Concept, character design, styling, 3D art

Methods
      Character research, historical reference, fashion remix, 3D modeling and rendering     Overview

    I have always been interested in the visual culture of old Vietnamese dynasties, especially the Nguyen Dynasty. At the same time, I did not want the project to stop at reproducing traditional references. I wanted to see what would happen if I brought that heritage into the pop culture and fashion language around me.

    That idea became Hoa Kieu: a Vietnamese American raised in the USA who accidentally travels back to the Nguyen Dynasty after trying on an ao yem inherited from her grandmother.  


Research Question     How can a fictional character use fashion remix to express the unstable relationship between inherited culture, diaspora identity, and contemporary self-image?  

Context     The idea came partly from friends who had lived abroad and gradually adopted an Asian Baby Girl - ABG style: strong lashes, defined brows, rebellious clothing, decorated nails, and a love of hypebeast items. I found it interesting that they could feel very international and still remain emotionally connected to Vietnam.

    Hoa Kieu carries the same contradiction. She knows little about ancient heritage, but she still has an affection for her country and people. Most of what she knows comes from family memory, fragments of culture, and the way she chooses to dress.  


Character Concept     When Hoa Kieu arrives in the Nguyen Dynasty, she is shocked by the way people dress. Instead of following the rules, she combines the ao yem and vay dup with ABG makeup, luxury accessories, hypebeast styling, and whatever she personally finds appealing. This is why I call her a “temporal criminal”: she enters history and immediately starts messing with it.  


Design Approach     I began with Nguyen Dynasty references such as traditional hairstyles, jewellery, and garments, then studied how Dong Ho and Hang Trong paintings simplify a figure while emphasizing its most recognizable features. For the modern side, I looked at hypebeast toys and the ways artists such as Hoang Thuy Linh and Suboi bring Vietnamese culture into contemporary styling.

    My first model did not feel Vietnamese enough, so I returned to the concept, changed the outfit to an ao yem and vay dup, redesigned familiar brand logos with Vietnamese motifs, and pushed the makeup, tattoos, handbag, and accessories further.  


Outcome     The final character does not belong completely to the past or the present. She uses fashion to show a hybrid, imperfect relationship with Vietnamese identity - one built through inheritance, distance, curiosity, and personal taste.  


Reflection     Through Hoa Kieu, I learned that cultural references do not have to stay untouched to be respected. For me, the interesting part was finding a balance where the character could be playful and rebellious while still carrying a real affection for where she comes from.
