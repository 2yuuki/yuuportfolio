Threads of Go Vap
An interactive typographic poster translating the spatial texture of Go Vap Market into scan-based imagery, motion, and responsive type.
Index




Type
      Interactive poster, typography, motion, spatial documentation

Year
      2024

Role
      Concept, visual research, 3D scanning, typographic system, interaction and motion design

Tools / Methods
      3D scan, TouchDesigner, typographic experimentation, neighborhood observation

Process Book below Overview

    Go Vap Market has always been part of my life because I have lived in Go Vap since I was born. As a child, I saw it so often that it became an ordinary background and I rarely paid attention to it. This project gave me a reason to return, look carefully, and reconnect with a place that had quietly held many of my childhood memories.

    I turned that experience into an interactive typographic poster built from real 3D scans of the market and responsive visuals in TouchDesigner.  


Research Question     How can interactive typography translate the sensory and spatial identity of a familiar neighborhood into a visual experience?  

Context     A passage from Invisible Cities stayed with me: “Desires are already memories.” It made me think about how a familiar place can hold different versions of us at the same time. I chose threads as the key visual because they could represent both the connections inside a marketplace and the way childhood memories become intertwined.  


Method     I walked through the full length of the market and scanned it on my phone with Scaniverse. My first plan was to turn the whole scene into a point cloud, but the file was too detailed and the result lost too much of the original place. Instead of forcing that idea, I rendered a panoramic camera journey through the scan in Blender.

    I converted the custom thread-like typeface into a 3D mesh, broke it into points in TouchDesigner, and experimented with mouse tracking, audio-reactive motion, and switching between “Go Vap” and “City of Memories.”  











PERFORMANCE




System     The final system combines the 3D journey and typography into one responsive surface. Viewers can manipulate the threads, make them react to sound, and switch between the two typographic states before the work is projected at a larger scale.  


Outcome     The final poster does not try to document the market perfectly. It presents the way I remember it: layered, moving, slightly incomplete, and tied to the feeling of growing up in Go Vap.  


Reflection     This project helped me see that typography can carry more than language. It can also hold movement, atmosphere, and a personal relationship with a place.
