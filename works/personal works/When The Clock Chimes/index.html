“Imagine a future in which humans evolve to survive in a world lacking oxygen.”

That thought sparked my story idea. It was inspired by the Climate Clock campaign, which counts down how long Earth can endure if we keep releasing our current daily carbon emissions.When the clock reaches zero, the planet’s damage will be beyond repair.

The story synopsis:
           In a desolate, post-apocalyptic world where plants have perished and oxygen is running out, a man tries to save the Earth by merging with a plant. As he records his journey, the camera reveals the reality of a toxic, crumbling city, with the Climate Clock ticking down the final hours.

           Arriving at his destination, he plants a seed that quickly begins to sprout, sending roots emerging from his hands and arms. For a brief moment, he believes he has succeeded in his experiment, but the relentless countdown clock ticks in the background, reminding him of reality. In a shattering realization, he understands the horrifying truth: he is not becoming a savior; he is burying himself alive, trapped in a haunting illusion of hope as time runs out.

I tried to approach this piece of work as a small message to everyone: before the time runs out, we should act immediately and protect our home—the Earth.
