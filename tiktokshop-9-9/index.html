TikTok Shop 9.9 Mega Sale
A high-velocity commercial campaign executed from shoot to full CGI master in just 12 days.
Index




Type
      Commercial Video, Post-Production

Year
      2024

Role
      Post Producer

Agency
      OneCut (Production House)     The Context

    TikTok Shop 9.9 is a mega-sale campaign designed to capture the highly dynamic and trend-driven energy of the platform. The project presented an extreme timeline: from the first day of shooting to the release of the final full-CGI master, the entire production spanned exactly 12 days.

The Execution & Role

    Operating as the Post Producer at OneCut, my role was to architect and manage an ultra-fast post-production pipeline without compromising visual fidelity.

    I coordinated cross-functional teams spanning offline editing, color grading, and complex CGI/VFX integration. This project demonstrates my ability to thrive under intense pressure, managing rigorous quality control and delivering high-end, algorithm-optimized digital assets at a speed that meets the demanding pace of modern e-commerce campaigns.
