Sony Alpha Vietnam — KOL Social Films
Two KOL-led social films shaped through a focused film treatment for Sony Alpha Vietnam.
Index


KOL Social Film 01.
KOL Social Film 02.


Type
      Social Films, Film Treatment

Year
      2026

Role
      Creative for Film Treatment

Client
      Sony - Alpha Vietnam

Agency
      Climax The Context

    The project required two KOL-led social films that could communicate Sony Alpha's image-making experience in a concise, platform-native format.

The Execution & Role

    As Creative for Film Treatment, I worked with the creative team to shape the films' concept, narrative direction, visual references, and treatment structure before production.
