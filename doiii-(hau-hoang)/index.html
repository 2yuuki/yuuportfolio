Hau Hoang ft. MisThy: Doiii
A vibrant music video production blending energetic choreography with dynamic graphic design and fast-paced set coordination.
Index

Official Music Video.


Type
      Music Video, Graphic Design, Production

Year
      2025

Role
      Director's Assistant & Lead Graphic Designer

Production
      Brunchie Asia     The Context

    "Doiii" is a major music video release by renowned Vietnamese creator Hau Hoang, featuring MisThy. The project demanded a highly vibrant and playful visual language, requiring seamless coordination between energetic choreography, multiple set transformations, and engaging on-screen graphics.

The Execution & Role

    Operating in a dual, multidisciplinary capacity as both Director's Assistant and Graphic Designer, I bridged the gap between physical film production and digital visual identity.

    On set, I worked closely with Director Kha Bao to orchestrate complex shooting schedules and maintain creative consistency across scenes in a fast-paced environment. Off set, I developed and oversaw the core graphic design assets, ensuring that the playful energy of the video was perfectly translated across all visual touchpoints.
