Nguyen Thu Trang, also known as yuuwouldnever, is a Vietnamese multidisciplinary visual designer whose practice investigates the beauty of everyday life through functional visual works. She makes work that serves a purpose: to open reflection, communicate social conditions, or give form to inner experience. Her creative process is introspective yet communicative.

Moving through images, motion, design systems, craft, and interactive technologies, Trang uses each medium as a way to depict, investigate, and unfold everyday life.

Her background in film production shapes the way she thinks about images dynamically—through motion, composition, rhythm, and story. Commercial pitching strengthened her ability to articulate ideas clearly, while producing and post-producing trained her to ground ambitious ideas in practical execution.
