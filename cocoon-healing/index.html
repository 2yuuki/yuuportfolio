Cocoon: Finding The Healing
A visual journey of restoration and self-care, brought to life through serene art direction.
Index


Official Commercial Video.




Type
      Commercial Video, Art Direction

Year
      2026

Role
      Art Director

Agency
      Command+Art (Production)     The Context

    "Finding The Healing" is a campaign that centers on tranquility, self-care, and natural restoration. Featuring talent Minh Thuy, the visual narrative was designed to evoke a deep sense of calm, perfectly aligning with Cocoon's ethos as a mindful, vegan beauty brand.

The Execution & Role

    Operating in a dynamic and fast-paced production environment, my role as Art Director was to construct an environment where the audience could visually "feel" the healing process.

    By carefully curating the set design, color palettes, and natural elements, I worked closely with the Director and the production team to maintain a consistent, soothing visual language from the physical set all the way through to the final camera lens.
